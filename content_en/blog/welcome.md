After years of building banking system architecture, most of what I've learned — the pitfalls, the hard-won insights — lived scattered across notes and chat logs. This site is where I organize them.

## What I'll write

- **Architecture**: ECIF, core banking, distributed architecture, and how Domain-Driven Design (DDD) lands in banking.
- **Banking**: payment clearing, interest and limits, accounts/cards, AML/KYC/CRS.
- **Data**: data governance, encryption and security, sharding, messaging and stream processing.

## Who it's for

Peers, engineers moving into fintech, and anyone curious about *why* banking systems are designed the way they are.
